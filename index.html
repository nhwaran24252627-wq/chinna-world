Shruthi... illa, en Magalee... 🤌❤️‍🩹 Nee enna evlo time indha letter kekkura nu enakku nalla nyabagam irukku 😒😂 “Enakku oru letter kudu, un feelings-ah ezhuthu, enna pathi enna ninaikkura nu sollu” nu nee pala vaati keturukka... appovellam naan summa escape aayiduven, “apram kudukuren” nu sollitu poiduven. Aana unakku theriyuma... indha letter ezhutha enakku words illa nu illa... actually, words irundhaalum, naan feel panradha exact-ah words-la solla mudiyuma nu bayama irundhudhu. Ippo dhaan thonudhu... nee ivlo naal ketta andha letter-ku idhudhaan correct time nu. College mudinjiduchu... internship-la first pesina andha person, daily life-la annoying pannina andha same person, ippo en kitta illa... romba dhooram. 🫂❄️ And maybe indha distance dhaan, nee en life-la evlo space occupy pannirukka nu enakku innum clear-ah kaamikudhu.

Namma first pesinadhu internship-la dhaane... appo namma life-la nee ivlo important person aagiduva nu naan konjam kooda imagine pannala. Oru normal conversation madhiri dhaan start aachu... aana konjam konjam-ah pesinom, understand panninom, tease panninom, annoy panninom... and somehow nee enna purinjukka aarambichitta. Adhu dhaan enakku romba special. Naan sometimes enna feel panren nu enakke explain panna mudiyama iruppen... aana nee somehow adha understand panniduva. Naan sollama irukkura vishayathayum nee notice pannura moments irukku. Adhu romba periya vishayam nu appo sollala... aana ippo distance-la irukkumbodhu dhaan adhu evlo precious nu puriyudhu. 🫀

College days-ah nenacha sirippu varudhu 😒😂 Namma rendu perum oruthara oruthar annoy pannama irundha days count panna mudiyuma nu therila. Nee enna irritate pannuva, naan unna irritate pannuven, unnecessary arguments, teasing, mokka conversations, random messages, random fights... sometimes “dei poda” level varaikum poi, konjam nerathula thirumba normal aayiduvom. 😂🤌 And honestly... naan miss panradhu periya events illa. Indha chinna chinna nonsense moments dhaan. Appo ordinary-a therinja moments... ippo memories aayiduchu. College-la irundha time-la “idhellam daily nadakkudhu dhaane” nu nenachen. Ippo dhaan puriyudhu... daily-nu ninaicha vishayangal dhaan oru naal “I wish I could experience that one more time” nu aagidum nu. ❤️‍🩹

College mudiyura varaikum namma idhellam ivlo seekiram mudinjidum nu nenachadhilla. Oru point-la college, internship, conversations, random meetings, teasing, seeing each other, everything just became a part of routine. And then suddenly... college finished. Ovorutharum ovoru direction. Life move aaganum. Responsibilities varanum. Distance varanum. Namma rendu perum same place-la irukka mudiyadhu. ❄️ Andha change romba silent-ah vandhuduchu. No big goodbye scene... no dramatic ending... just one day namma regular life itself changed. Adhu dhaan konjam painful. Because sometimes the biggest endings don't even feel like endings when they happen.

Unakku oru vishayam honest-ah sollanum, Magalee... nee enakku evlo important nu unakku theriyuma nu enakku theriyadhu. Nee enna same way-la miss panriya nu theriyadhu. Naan un mind-la sometimes varena nu kooda theriyadhu. Naan unakku evlo close person nu nee feel panra nu enakku theriyadhu. And honestly, idhuku answer enakku venum nu force panna kooda naan virumbala. Because feelings-ku measurement illa. Aana en side-la mattum oru truth romba clear... nee en life-la romba special. 🫀 Nee enna understand pannina way, en kooda irundha way, ennoda stupid side-ah tolerate pannina way, enna annoy pannina way... ellame enakku value irukku. Maybe naan adha daily sollama irundhiruppen. Maybe sometimes naan care pannave maata madhiri behave panniruppen 😒😂. But inside... I cared. More than I probably showed.

Sometimes naan yosippen... “Namma college-la irundha days thirumba kidaicha enna panniruppen?” Maybe same nonsense dhaan panniruppen. 😂 Same teasing. Same annoying. Same random conversations. Same “nee poda” and “seri po” moments. But one thing mattum different-ah panniruppen... I would have noticed the moments more. Oru normal day nu kadandhu pogama, “one day I'm going to miss this” nu appove understand panniruppen. Un kooda pesina random five minutes, corridor-la paatha oru moment, unnecessary argument, sudden laugh... ivlo small things dhaan ippo enakku perusa feel aagudhu. 🫂

Ippo nee romba dhoorathula irukka... sila samayam unmaiyave unna miss panren. Edho dramatic movie madhiri illa... random-aana chinna chinna moments-la dhaan. Edhavadhu nadakkum, “idha Shruthi-kitta sollanum” nu thonum. Oru mokka joke varum, udane share panna thonum. Edhavadhu manasa disturb pannum, un kitta pesina nalla irukkum nu thonum. Oru memory varum, smile varum... appuram konjam silence. Yenna nee munnaadi irundha madhiri ippo inga illa. ❄️

Oruvarai miss panradhu-na idhu dhaan pola. Avanga namma mind-la every second irukkanum nu avasiyam illa... aana sila chinna moments-la thideernu, “ava inga irundha nalla irukkume” nu thonum. 🫂❤️‍🩹

Magalee... naan un life-la enna place occupy panren nu enakku theriyadhu. Oruvela naan mukkiyamaanavan-aa irukkalam. Oruvela nee nesikkira neraya perla oruthan mattum-aa irukkalam. Oruvela sila samayam nee enna pathi ninaikkave maatta. Enakku nijama theriyadhu.

Aana adha pathi enakku kavala illa madhiri nadikka naan virumbala. Enakku irukku. Yenna naan unna avlo value panren. 🤌🫀

Naan feel panra madhiriye neeyum feel pannanum nu expect pannala. Enakku kudukkura importance-a neeyum same-a kudukkanum nu expect pannala. Indha letter unakku pressure-a maaranum nu kooda naan virumbala. Naan ivlo naal jokes pinnadi olinjikama, en manasula romba naala irundha vishayatha oru thadava sollanum nu dhaan nenachen.

Nee en life-la vandhadhu oru coincidence-a irundhirukkalam... aana nee en kooda irundhadhu coincidence madhiri feel aagala. ❤️‍🩹 Internship conversations-um college madness-um nadula, nee illaama irukradha naan nijama feel panna mudinja oruthiy aayitta. Adhanala dhaan indha letter ezhudha enakku ivlo naal aachu. Yenna naan ezhudhina apram idhu nijam aagidum. Naan sonna apram “summa sonnen” illa “joke pannadha” nu solli thappikka mudiyadhu. 😒😂 So, innikku naan idha proper-a solren.

Nee enakku just oru friend nu solli mudichida mudiyala. Unna naan annoy pannuven, unna kooda sirippen, loosu madhiri iruppen, un kooda free-a iruppen... edho naan proper-a explain pannama irukkura ennoda sila parts-a nee purinjikkira madhiri irukka. 🫂

Oru aal kitta eppovum perfect-a irukkanum nu thevai illama irukradhu romba azhagana vishayam. Sila samayam namma irritating-a, silent-a, loosu-a, kovama, santhoshama, random-a iruppom... aana appadiyum somehow namma namma-va iruppom. Adhu dhaan unna pathi naan romba value panra vishayam.

Sila samayam future pathiyum yosippen. Life namma enga kondu pogum nu theriyadhu. Oruvela busy aayiduvom. Different cities-la vaazhvom. Ippo irukkura alavukku namma pesama irukkalam. Maasangal illa varushangal kalichu meet pannitu, evlo maaritom nu sirikkalam. Life namma expect pannadha madhiri surprise pannalam. Enakku theriyadhu.

Aana oru vishayam mattum unmaiyave aasai... namma strangers aagakoodadhu. 🫂 Un life-la “Daddy” irukkura oru chinna corner eppovum irukkanum... en life-la en “Magalee” irukkura oru chinna corner eppovum irukkanum. 🫀

Unna en journey-la venum. Future epdi irukkum nu enakku therinjadhaal illa... enakku theriyadhu. Aana years kazhichum naan therinjirukkanum nu ninaikkira peroda list-la un pera dhaan naturally varudhu.

Nee neraya vishayam achieve panradha paakanum. Un mokka problems ellam kekkanum. Un wins-a celebrate pannanum. Namma vayasaagi, supposedly mature aanaalum unna annoy pannanum. 😂🤌 Oru naal, years kazhichu, namma indha college days-a thirumbi paathu, “Dei... namma evlo loosu-ah irundhom” nu sollanum. 😒😂 Appuram munnaadi madhiriye sirikkanum.

Oruvela oru naal, naan pakkathula illadha nerathula illa nan intha ulagathula illatha nerathula nee indha letter-a thirumba padippiya. Oru random place-la, oru random day-la irundhu, thideernu enna nyabagam pannuva. Appo... smile pannu. Sad-a feel pannadha because nan illanalum unna gaurdian angel aa irunthu paathutu irupan nee happy aa irukiyaa illiya nu . 🫂

Un life-la edho oru time-la, unmaiyave unna care pannina, unakku related-aana chinna chinna vishayangal kooda nyabagam vechikitta, nee happy-a irukkanum nu dhaan aasai patta, life evlo maarinaalum nee avanoda journey-la oru part-aa irukkanum nu manasukulla aasai patta oruthan irundhan nu nyabagam vechikko.

Nee enna miss panriya illaya nu enakku theriyadhu... aana naan unna miss panren, Magalee. ❤️‍🩹 Sila samayam naan othukkura alavukku mela. Sila samayam romba chinna chinna vishayangal-la. Sila samayam unnai nyabagam paduthura edhavadhu kekkumbodhu. Sila samayam college nyabagam varumbodhu. Sila samayam hours-a ukkandhu nonsense-a pesanum nu thonumbodhu. 🫂❄️

Distance enakku oru vishayam puriya vechudhu... sila peroda presence everyday guaranteed-a irukkadhu nu theriyumbodhu dhaan, avanga evlo precious nu puriyudhu.

And Shruthi... if you ever wondered why I sometimes care so much, why I remember certain things, why I don't want this friendship to disappear with college... this is why. 🫀 You matter to me. More than my casual words show. More than my stupid teasing shows. More than I probably know how to explain.

You asked me many times for this letter. 😒😂 Maybe you expected it earlier. Maybe you thought I'd never actually write it. But I wanted to wait until I could give you something that wasn't just a few lines copied from somewhere. I wanted it to be mine. My words. My memories. My confusion. My feelings. My fear of losing someone important. My hope that we stay. Everything.

So here it is, Magalee... finally. 🤌❤️‍🩹
